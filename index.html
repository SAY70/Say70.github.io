About

Postdoctoral Fellow

  • Affiliation/s: Mississippi State University
  • Email: surajspce@gmail.com

My research lies at the intersection of computational electromagnetics, remote sensing, and environmental modeling. As a lifelong enthusiast in sensor analytics, I focus on the design, optimization, and calibration of radar and optical sensor architectures to establish biophysically and computationally informed sensing ecosystems. I develop electromagnetic scattering models and sensor fusion frameworks that integrate SAR, GNSS, multispectral, hyperspectral, and LiDAR observations to model forest and crop canopy structure, soil moisture dynamics, and radiative transfer processes. Additionally, my work emphasizes physics-guided simulations, voxelized LiDAR analytics, and microwave scattering theory to enable accurate biophysical parameter retrieval and scalable monitoring of vegetation systems for precision agriculture and forest ecosystem assessment.
